PromptMask Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 6, 2026

PromptMask does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All processing happens locally on your device.

Local storage is used only to cache a model file on your device between sessions. No prompt text, personal information, or browsing history is ever written to storage or sent anywhere.

We do not sell or share any data with third parties.
